# Elementary privacy policy — draft

Effective date: [release date]

Elementary is a Bible reading application for Windows published by Grant Street Design.

## Information stored on your device

Elementary stores your selected translation, reading position, recent reading history, display preferences, and reading-streak progress in the app’s local Windows storage. This information is used only to provide app features and is not transmitted to the publisher.

Elementary does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, payment information, or reading notes.

## Internet access

The included Bible translations are available offline.

[Choose one of the following sections before publication.]

### If Verse of the Day is not included in 1.0

Elementary 1.0 does not send your reading activity or app settings to an online service.

### If the network-backed Verse of the Day feature is included

When you request Verse of the Day, Elementary makes HTTPS requests to the Bible.org and Unsplash services to obtain a verse and optional background image. Those services may receive your IP address and standard network request information. Unsplash may also receive image-view or download interaction data as described in its API terms and privacy policy.

Elementary does not include your reading history, selected chapters, streak data, or app settings in those requests.

Third-party policies:

- Bible.org: https://bible.org/privacy
- Unsplash: https://unsplash.com/privacy

## Sharing and sale

The publisher does not sell your personal information and does not share your on-device reading data with advertisers or data brokers.

## Retention and deletion

Your settings, reading history, and streak data remain in the app’s local Windows storage until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

## Security

Network requests, when enabled, use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app limits the data it handles to what is needed for its features.

## Children

Elementary is a general-audience scripture reader and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when Elementary’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
